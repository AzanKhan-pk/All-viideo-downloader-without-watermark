FAQ

Is this the original project?

Yes. The platform builds are based on the existing repository rather than a redesigned downloader.

What can I download?

Only media you have permission to download, public-domain material, or content whose terms authorize downloading.

Why might a URL fail?

A source can be private, restricted, DRM-protected, unsupported, or temporarily unavailable.

Privacy Policy

The packaged desktop app processes downloads on the user's computer. The download website does not require an account. Normal hosting infrastructure may receive standard web request information such as IP address and browser metadata.

Terms & Usage

Use the application only for media you are legally allowed to download. Do not use it to bypass DRM, access private material without permission, or infringe copyright. The developer is not responsible for misuse by users.
